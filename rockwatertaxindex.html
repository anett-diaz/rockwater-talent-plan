30 Days: Foundation 🚀

Establish recruiting infrastructure, train your team on interview standards, and launch multi-channel sourcing. Foundation is built.

1. Recruiting Process Audit & Streamline

Problem: LinkedIn + emails + manual note-passing = candidates falling through cracks, zero visibility, no feedback structure.

  • Map current recruiting flow end-to-end: Sourcing → Screening → Interview → Offer
  • Set up tracking system with clear stages: Sourced | Recruiter Screen | Hiring Manager Screen | Final Round | Offer | Closed
  • Create automated candidate email templates for screening confirmation, interview scheduling, rejection
  • Move all candidates into ATS dashboard — no more random emails
You can't scale what you can't see. This is your foundation.

2. Build Candidate Pipeline for Immediate Open Roles

Problem: You need to hire 15-20 people by EOY. That pipeline doesn't exist yet — it's scattered across LinkedIn, inbound, and referrals.

  • Immediately source candidates for open roles: Director of Accounting Services, Tax Paralegal, CPA, Sales
  • Multi-channel approach: LinkedIn + targeted job boards + warm referrals
  • Set up outreach cadence (HeyReach, Lemlist) for passive candidates — personalized, templated, trackable
  • Build pipeline depth: aim for 3-5x pipeline for each open role by end of month
Multi-channel sourcing beats single-channel every time.

End of 30 Days Checkpoint

  • Recruiting process mapped + candidates flowing through ATS
  • Hiring managers and interviewers trained on interviewing best practices and feedback standards
  • Multi-channel sourcing in motion for key roles
  • Weekly CEO/leadership sync established for pipeline overview with analytics and data to improve for next week

60 Days: Process Embedding & Program Planning ⚙️

Lock in recruiting operations, build automated onboarding, and establish people programs. The system becomes reliable and repeatable.

1. Recruiting Process Operationalization

Problem: System is built but needs to stick. Consistency required to scale without quality degradation.

  • By day 60, all candidates flow through standardized pipeline — no exceptions, no random emails
  • All interviewers have completed training and been formally signed off
  • Establish weekly recruiting sync with CEO: pipeline health, blockers, metrics
  • Automate rejection emails (respectful, on-brand)
  • Track metrics: candidates per stage, time-to-hire by role, offer acceptance rate
When everyone follows the same process, you catch quality issues early and scale without chaos.

2. Onboarding Audit & Agentic Layer

Problem: New hires need tech setup, team onboarding, morale boosters in critical first 2 weeks. This is manual and ad-hoc right now.

  • Interview all recent hires and staff members: what worked, what was confusing, what fell through?
  • Audit current onboarding checklist or build from scratch
  • Map tasks that can be automated: tech provisioning, welcome emails, first-week calendar, team intros
  • Build agentic workflow using Slack + Google Sheets or Notion (when hire is confirmed → automatic sequence)
First 2 weeks are critical for retention. Smooth onboarding signals you care. Automation handles repetition.

3. People Programs Roadmap

Problem: People programs are an afterthought. They should signal that Rockwater cares about people, not just headcount.

  • Audit: what HR programs exist today? (onboarding, performance reviews, 1:1 cadence, career pathing?)
  • Identify gaps: structured performance conversations, career development, team rituals
  • Draft quarterly people programs calendar: Q4 onboarding + AI training + EOY event | Q1 performance reviews | Q2 AI training series
People programs signal you care about people. Early signal matters for retention.

End of 60 Days Checkpoint

  • Recruiting pipeline is operationalized + consistent
  • All interviewers trained + consistently following standards
  • Agentic onboarding workflow live
  • New hires report smoother first 2 weeks
  • People programs calendar socialized + Q4 events planned

Key Principles 🔑

Five principles guide how this work gets done.

1. Speed & Urgency

You need 15-20 hires by EOY. Move fast, iterate, don't over-engineer. A 90% solution deployed today beats a 100% solution that never ships.

2. Hands-On

She'll be sourcing, interviewing, and onboarding candidates herself. Automation handles repetition, not judgment. You need someone in the trenches who can see what's working in real time.

3. Transparency

Weekly sync on pipeline, metrics, blockers, and wins. You stay looped in. No surprises. This builds trust and makes her your partner, not just your hire.

4. Buyer Education

Your hiring managers need to understand why scorecards matter, why consistent feedback matters, why the process works. Buy-in drives quality. Without it, the system breaks.

5. Culture Signal

People programs signal that Rockwater cares about people, not just headcount. Early signal matters for retention. A strong onboarding and EOY celebration show you value your team.
